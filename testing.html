Join the Bilingua Tutor closed test

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Bilingua Tutor is an AI language tutor with a hand-written grammar map for Dutch, German, Finnish, Hungarian and English. Before it goes live on Google Play, Google requires a closed test with real people on real phones. You would be one of them.

Email us to sign up

The button opens your email app with a short message to fill in. We reply with the invite and the opt-in link. Spaces are limited and invites go out in the order we get them.

What we ask of you

  1. Email us the Google account (Gmail address) you use in the Play Store on your Android phone, your phone model and Android version, and the language you are learning.
  2. Join the test group from the invite we send you, using that same Google account.
  3. Open the opt-in link from the invite and tap "Become a tester".
  4. Install the app from Google Play using the link on that page.
  5. Keep it installed and stay opted in for at least 14 days, and open it a few times. Google counts this, so please do not uninstall early.
  6. Tell us what you find: through the feedback option on the Play test page, or by email to kalmariumservices@gmail.com.

Good to know before you join

  • You need an Android phone (Android 8 or newer) and a Google account. There is no iPhone version.
  • The grammar map and reviews work straight away. The AI tutor can run on your phone with a one-time download of about 2.6 GB over Wi-Fi, which needs a recent arm64 phone with about 6 GB of RAM. Or you can connect an AI provider with your own key.
  • Lucy is an AI and can be wrong. Her answers are labelled, and you can report an answer inside the app.
  • Testers get the AI unlock for free. During the test you will not be charged.
  • If you use a cloud AI provider with your own key, that provider bills you for usage. The on-device model costs nothing.

What happens to your email

We use the address you send only to add you to the test and to write to you about it. It is not shared with anyone else, and we delete it after the test ends. Google receives your Google account address as part of running the closed test, under Google's own privacy policy. See our privacy policy for how the app itself handles data.
